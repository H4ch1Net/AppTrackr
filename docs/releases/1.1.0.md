AppTrackr 1.1.0 is a full rework: more accurate tracking, a new interface with six themes, and in-app updates from now on. Your existing history, rewards, village and settings all carry over.

## How to update

**If you have AppTrackr 1.0.x**

1. Download **AppTrackr_Setup.exe** below.
2. Run it. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. Click through the installer. It closes the running AppTrackr and replaces it in the same place.
4. AppTrackr starts again with all your data.

Before converting your data, AppTrackr saves a copy of it as `data-backup-schema0-<date>.sqlite` in `%APPDATA%\AppTrackr`.

**If you use the portable zip**

Download **AppTrackr_Portable.zip**, extract it anywhere and run `AppTrackr.exe`. Your data is shared with the installed version, so nothing is lost.

**After this update**

AppTrackr checks for new versions once a day. When one is out, open **Settings** and click **Download and install**.

## What's new

- Time counts only while an app is in front, stops at your last input when you go idle, and is saved every 30 seconds. Days that 1.0 under-counted (sessions that ended in idle or ran past midnight) are recalculated from your history.
- A new interface: live session dial, calendar heatmap, per-app history, and "In other terms" (your week as Everest summit days, Moon trips or marathons).
- Six themes (Graphite, Carbon, Midnight, Paper, Porcelain, Sage) with eight accent colours.
- Daily limits per app, exclusions with undo, pause for a set time, notifications you can choose by type.
- Rewards and village carry over; claiming and building now have a little celebration.
- CSV and JSON export, backup and restore, start in the tray at sign-in.
