AppTrackr 1.2.0 turns the rewards into an idle game that pays you for staying focused, adds a floating timer over other apps, and rewrites "In other terms". Your history, village, level, streak and settings all carry over.

## How to update

**If you have AppTrackr 1.1.0**

Open **Settings** and click **Download and install**. AppTrackr closes, updates and starts again.

**If you have AppTrackr 1.0.x, or the button does not show up**

1. Download **AppTrackr_Setup.exe** below.
2. Run it. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. Click through the installer. It replaces the old version in the same place and keeps your data.

**If you use the portable zip**

Download **AppTrackr_Portable.zip**, extract it over the old folder (or anywhere) and run `AppTrackr.exe`. Your data is shared with the installed version.

## What's new

- **Focus game.** Pick your focus apps (an editor, a design tool, docs). Unbroken time in them builds flow: each minute is worth more the longer you stay, from x1 up to x3 after 75 minutes. A quick look at chat does not break it.
- **Village that works while you do.** Lumberyards, quarries, farms, mines and labs turn focus into resources. Collect the harvest, then build and upgrade.
- **Daily focus goal.** Reach it (2 h by default, change it in Settings) to grow your streak and open a chest.
- **Floating timer.** A small clock in the corner shows how long you have been in the current app, and your flow tier in focus apps. Drag it anywhere, double-click to open AppTrackr, right-click to hide it, or turn it off in Settings. It stays out of the way of full-screen games and videos.
- **In other terms, rewritten.** Plain comparisons you can picture, like movies watched, books read or flights taken, instead of percentages.

## Notes for existing players

- Apps that earned rewards before are your focus apps now. If you had none, your favorites become focus apps once.
- Rewards you had not claimed yet are still on the Focus page.
- Buildings, resources, level, XP, credits and your streak are kept. Storage now holds more.
